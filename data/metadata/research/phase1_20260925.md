## Net-return tournament

The frozen candidate slate was equal weight, momentum-20, and reversal-1 with a common trailing-liquidity universe, next-open fills, commissions, spread, impact, borrow, financing, and identical initial capital. The run records complete daily and fill ledgers and also replays double impact. Validation selected momentum-20 by mean net return; the test phase retained that selection. The table shows configured-impact cumulative simulated net price returns, including all modeled costs. These are not live returns.

| Candidate | Validation (495 dates) | Test (431 dates) | Test rejected orders |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Equal weight | +2.22% | +36.56% | 0 |
| Momentum-20 | −2.26% | +22.74% | 4 |
| Reversal-1 | −28.87% | +0.95% | 23 |

On the test set, momentum-20's mean daily differential versus equal weight was −2.286 bps; the double-impact differential was −2.299 bps. The full-slate Reality Check p-value was 0.946, SPA p-value 1.000, and selected candidate's StepM adjusted p-value 0.951. Every trial completed terminal liquidation, but the selected strategy failed the double-impact superiority requirement. `selected_holdout_adjusted_rejection=false`, `economic_evidence_gate=false`, `promote=false`, and `live_pnl_claim=false`. This is a **negative economic result**, and the entire slate remains disclosed.

The run assumes raw opens and closes are comparable share units. Dividends, splits, actual borrow locates, auction depth, and exchange-specific fills are not modeled; cost and impact inputs are scenarios rather than measured venue estimates. A previously inspected, survivor-selected data pool cannot establish independent performance.
